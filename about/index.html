Keke Long

Dr. Long is an incoming Assistant Professor in the Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering at Rutgers University–New Brunswick. Her research focuses on advancing safe and reliable intelligent transportation systems (ITS) through physics-enhanced AI. Her work spans automated vehicle perception, decision-making, and safety evaluation, as well as intelligent transportation monitoring and management, with an emphasis on real-world applications.

Dr. Long has served as co-PI on a federally funded transportation research project and as an instructor in the CCAT certificate program. She develops AI-based methods that incorporate physical knowledge to improve interpretability and adaptability to changing conditions. She validates these methods through simulation, digital twins, and real-vehicle experiments to support the reliable deployment of automated transportation systems. She has published more than 30 papers, including articles in Transportation Research Parts A, C, and E, IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems, and IEEE Intelligent Transportation Systems Magazine.
